Verity Report
VR-2941-A

"The Great Wall of China is visible from space with the naked eye."

✗ FALSE
Confidence Score 94 / 100

Summary

This claim is false. While the Great Wall is undeniably large, its width (~5–8 metres) is too narrow to resolve with the naked eye from low Earth orbit (~400 km altitude). NASA and multiple astronaut accounts confirm this is a persistent myth with no supporting evidence.

Reasoning Chain

🔍
Researcher 0.95 confidence
Human visual acuity at 400 km altitude cannot resolve objects narrower than ~10 km. The Great Wall averages 5–8 m wide. NASA's FAQs explicitly state the wall is not visible from the ISS without optical aids. No credible astronaut has confirmed unaided visibility. Sources: NASA.gov FAQ, Scientific American, peer-reviewed studies on human visual limits.
⚔️
Skeptic 0.92 confidence
Counter-argument: Chinese astronaut Yang Liwei reportedly stated he could not see it during his 2003 mission. The claim persists despite repeated debunking — potentially due to textbook propagation. There is no verified photographic evidence of unaided naked-eye sighting.
⚖️
Bias Detector 0.88 confidence
Mild nationalist framing: the claim inflates national achievement mythology. No significant political motivation to actively spread the myth today, but institutional inertia in older educational materials has sustained it. Bias toward the claim is low; no major ideological agenda detected.
🔗
Source Cross-Checker 0.96 confidence
High source consistency across: NASA.gov, Snopes.com, Scientific American, ESA documentation, and peer-reviewed optics research. All independently reach the same conclusion. No credible primary source supports the affirmative. Source reliability: very high.

Key Sources

  • NASA.gov — FAQs on the Great Wall
  • Scientific American — Myth Debunked
  • Vision Research Journal — Visual Acuity Study
  • Snopes.com — Fact Check
